1. PROBLEM & WORLD (BUILD FOR YOUR WORLD):
   - Night-shift commercial cleaners do essential work at 2 a.m. with zero visibility, no portable proof of skill, and paper training binders nobody opens.
   - Building service contractors (BSCs) lose contracts over "they didn't clean" disputes and spend hours driving between sites for re-checks.

2. COMMERCIAL STATION CHECK-IN & LOCKED TIMED RUN:
   - Cleaners check in via a registered site picker and Version-2 QR sticker scanner across 10 building sectors, 13 facility areas, and 28 cleaning spots.
   - After snapping an in-app Before photo, a locked 1:30 countdown runs while a neural host voice hypes the cleaner every 6–8 seconds over a 122 BPM WebAudio house groove in English, Mexican Spanish, or Polish.
   - Tapping Complete Challenge automatically captures the After photo and submits in one step.

3. TWO-RUNG AI VERIFICATION (ON-DEVICE + META CLOUD & OPEN WEIGHTS):
   - Rung 1 (On-Device, Zero-Key): MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0 station mismatch gate (verifyStationEnvironment) + object clearance matching, 96×72 structural edge correlation, 3×3 luminance zone analyzer, and Pose Landmarker Lite style scoring.
   - Rung 2 (Meta Model API + Baseten Spark Open): Muse Spark 1.3 (api.meta.ai) and Muse Spark Open (meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026 on Baseten) produce per-surface scorecards with 0.6 confidence gating, SAM 3.1 surface bounding-box overlays, and Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 spoken-note transcription, logged to an exportable Provider Run Log JSON.

4. EXPLAINABLE 50/25/25 SCORECARD, DAILY DECK & DUAL LEADERBOARD:
   - Every run is explained across Visible Cleanliness (50%), Technique (25%), and Benchmark Time (25% with +15s dwell credits), with a 1-tap Dispute button and zero admin score editing.
   - Includes a 30-card trilingual Daily Swipe Deck and a Dual-Mode Leaderboard (Shift Challenge + Daily Deck) plus supervisor tools (Panes 11–15).
