⚠️ Read First · Disqualification & Pass/Fail Guardrails

Five Non-Negotiable Submission Rules

  • 1. Mandatory First Sentence in the 3-Minute Video (Pre-Existing vs. Event-Built Work): The hackathon rules require opening the demo video with one clear line separating pre-existing work from what was built during the event, and showcasing only event-built work. Say verbatim: "CleanQuest started as a basic single-screen camera timer prototype; everything we are showing today — the 15-screen trilingual MVP, MECE JanSan taxonomy, Muse Spark 1.3 verified scorecards, SAM 3.1 segmentation overlays, Muse voice-note transcription, Baseten Spark Open provider + audit log, 650+ neural voice clips, 122 BPM WebAudio synth, AAA Daily Swipe Deck, and Dual-Mode Esports Leaderboard — was built during this hackathon."
  • 2. Never Frame CleanQuest as a "Simple Image Analyzer": "Simple image analyzers" are on the organizers' pass/fail anti-pattern list. Always present CleanQuest as a closed-loop gamified work-verification platform, daily trilingual training deck, and portable cleaner-owned career passport + geofenced inspection record.
  • 3. Strict Product Language Rules: (a) The game-show host voice hypes and celebrates only — it never instructs or coaches technique during a run; (b) CleanQuest scores visible cleanliness and technique only — never claim sanitation or disinfection verification; (c) A benchmark time is never a certification; (d) Never name external trade associations in the repo or UI (use generic "standards body").
  • 4. Exact README Phrase Rule: The phrase powered by Muse at scale must appear exactly once in README.md (in the intro) and nowhere else in the repository. (Already verified: count = 1).
  • 5. Zero Secrets in Git: API keys (Meta Model API, Baseten, OpenRouter, Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq) are entered only in the browser's Settings ⚙ screen (stored in localStorage) and never committed to the repository.

PROBLEM & WORLD (BUILD FOR YOUR WORLD)
Commercial night cleaners do essential, physically demanding work at 2 a.m. that nobody sees — receiving zero recognition, no portable proof of skill when changing jobs, and paper binders of training that nobody opens. Meanwhile, building service contractors (BSCs) lose contracts over "they didn't clean" disputes and spend hours driving between sites for re-checks.

WHAT CLEANQUEST DOES
CleanQuest is a closed-loop gamified work-verification platform, daily trilingual training deck (English, Mexican Spanish, and Polish), and portable cleaner-owned career record:
1. Check-In & MECE JanSan Station Assignment: Cleaners check in via a registered site picker and Version-2 QR sticker scanner across a MECE JanSan taxonomy of 10 commercial building sectors, 13 facility areas, and 28 cleaning spots.
2. Timed Run & Live 9-Zone Vision Telemetry: A cleaner snaps an in-app Before photo and cleans against a locked 1:30 benchmark clock while an energetic game-show host hypes them every 6–8 seconds over a 122 BPM WebAudio house groove. During the run, on-device vision samples a 36×27 luma grid across 9 surface zones and captures mid-run progress snapshots. Tapping Complete Challenge automatically captures the After photo and submits in one step.
3. Two-Rung AI Verification Ladder (On-Device + Meta Cloud & Open Weights):
   - Rung 1 (On-Device, Zero-Key, Private by Construction): MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0 runs an Environment & Station Mismatch Gate (verifyStationEnvironment) plus bounding-box object clearance matching, paired with a 96×72 structural edge correlation check, a 3×3 luminance gradient analyzer, and MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite for victory-pose style scoring.
   - Rung 2 (Meta Model API & Baseten Spark Open): Downscaled 768px Before/After JPEG pairs are graded by Muse Spark 1.3 (api.meta.ai) or Muse Spark Open (meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026 on Baseten) into structured per-surface scorecards with 0.6 confidence gating, grounded in pixels by SAM 3.1 surface bounding-box overlays, with spoken notes transcribed via Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 (muse-voice-transcribe-1.0). Every Baseten/cloud run is recorded in an exportable Provider Run Log JSON.
4. Explainable 50/25/25 Scorecard & 1-Tap Appeals: Every score is explained across three bars — Visible Cleanliness (50%), Technique (25%), and Benchmark Time (25%, with +15s chemical dwell credits never penalizing speed) — accompanied by a 5-Tier musical stinger, gapless Kokoro-82M spoken percentage readout, and a 1-tap Dispute button. No admin, supervisor, or sponsor can ever edit a score.
5. AAA Daily Swipe Deck & Dual-Mode Esports Leaderboard: Cleaners swipe 5 brand-neutral training cards per day (with widescreen SVG dioramas, missed-card repeat, and post-answer labeled sponsor slots) and compete on both the Shift Challenge Leaderboard and the Daily Deck Leaderboard, while supervisors manage sites, QR stickers, 5-seat squads, and 5-day training packs.
